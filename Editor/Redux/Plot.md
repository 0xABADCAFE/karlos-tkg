# AB3D2 TKG Redux Plotline Ideas

Work in progress

## Background

### Escape

Commander Reynolds, sole human survivor of the Osiris III incident has escaped in an emergency shuttle.

* The desperate launch and lack of preparation means the shuttle has broken free of the planetary gravity well but does not have sufficient reaction mass left to correct the current trajectory and enter a stable parking orbit around the planet.  
* The ship is now in a long elliptical orbit around Osiris.  
* Ill-equipped for a prolonged flight, he is left with no choice but to record a brief distress message, reconfigure the systems for minimum power operation and go into the hibernation pod.  
* At minimum operating conditions, energy reserves should last at most 80-100 days.

### Rescue

Unknown to Reynolds, the *Indomitable* was already in the system.

* Running dark, no transponder or other active emissions.  
* Long-range sensors and network inserts had been collecting data since well before the outbreak.  
* Dispatched by the *EAB* (Exoplanet Assetization Bloc), *de facto* owners of the system weeks before the outbreak.  
  * Mission to observe, contain the outbreak and if necessary, destroy all traces, including the facility responsible.  
  * The escape shuttle was unexpected but also subject to a contingency plan.

Having completed its primary mission, the *Indomitable* interdicts the escape shuttle and recovers the unconscious Reynolds. Specific orders are that Reynolds should be:

* Quarantined and kept in hibernation.  
* Shipped to *LV-612* for "enhanced debrief".

### EAB

The Exoplanet Assetization Bloc is the umbrella organisation that encompasses the largest corporations that grew out of humanity’s original interplanetary expansion and eventual interstellar exploration boom. The consolidated power, wealth and influence of the organisation grants it the status of an independent interstellar government, without the bureaucracy and accountability of its democratic counterparts elsewhere.

## Setting

*Redux* takes place in the *Set* system. Named for the brother (and murderer) of *Osiris* in ancient Egyptian mythology, *Set* is an unremarkable orange K-class star, situated approximately at the centre of a group of *assetized* star systems spanning approximately 80 light years through the longest axis. The exact location of the star cluster is highly classified, but Set is believed to be a relatively young system by Sol standards.

### LV-612

*LV-612*, aka *Set II* is the second of five planets in the *Set* system, a minimally habitable “super Mars”:

* Terrestrial planet with a viable atmosphere and 70% Earth gravity.   
* Relatively water-scarce, dotted with lakes and small seas in lower depressions, otherwise mostly rocky desert, but with significant ice deposits are the poles.  
* Native life is restricted to primitive, mostly harmless unicellular life living in or close to water.

The planet serves as the central hub for the EAB’s Advanced Projects Division (APD). Planetwide installations are intentionally placed far apart to enhance physical isolation and containment for sites dealing with high-risk projects. Facilities are generally self-sufficient and connected by a teleporter network.  The Indomitable arrives at LV-612 and docks with one of the major low-orbit stations that support surface to orbit teleportation.

## Levels

The exact order of later levels is not fully decided.

### Level A: The Awakening

Some undisclosed length of time after the arrival at LV-612, Reynolds awakens in the quarantined medical bay of the Indomitable. Unaware of the Indomitable's original mission, he assumes he has been rescued.

Level begins in the medical bay:

* Basic blaster and shotgun located close by.  
* The immediate area introduces collectables, locks, doors, crouching and jumping concepts.  
* Wall panel displays show that a lockdown is in effect.  
* Soon it becomes obvious the ship has been infiltrated by the breed.

Beyond the quarantine isolation door an atrium section contains an (almost) fully-sealed isolation bulkhead door preventing access to the forward teleport section. The level exit doors connect to one of the umbilicals through which the ship is attached to the station, which requires a key. The only other route is through a systems section that follows the original level layout.

* Reynolds must find the key to open this door to complete the level.  
* Original level access to the bridge is a dead end having another isolation door, fully engaged.  
* A locked door requiring a red keycard provides an alternative route to the forward section of the main atrium.  
* The concepts of door keys, bridges and teleports are introduced here.

The ship's bridge section on the flight deck is now only accessible by teleport:

* Further situational information is revealed.  
* Displays reveal the current location (LV-612) and a drone view of the docking ring.  
* The armoury section is initially locked.  
* Completion of the flight deck section permits teleportation back to the systems section.  
* Introduces the concept of secret areas and some slightly more challenging enemies.

### Level B: Docking Ring

Exiting Level A leads through the docking umbilical to one of the numerous levels in the docking ring of the orbital station. The overall layout of the original level is retained, but reimagined as a human built structure. 

* The initial area of the level visually matches the exit of the preceding level to maintain continuity.  
* Situational displays reveal further clues as to what has happened:  
  * One quarter of the ring has been sealed and vented to space, suggesting heavy damage to that area.  
  * The station is running on auxiliary power and will soon deorbit due to atmospheric drag if primary power is not restored.  
* Remaining entrances to the inner maintenance and cargo handling sections of the ring are locked, requiring a key to open.  
* Key lowers a lift in the cargo section and also unlocks the doors that block access to the inner sections of the level.  
* Introduces new robotic enemy classes.  
* Includes additional secret areas and mechanisms, e.g. shot activated doors.  
* Escape is only possible after taking down the heavy mechanoid. The exit shaft is now a maintenance duct that leads to the engineering section of the station.

### Level C: Infestation

Begins at the bottom of the maintenance shaft in the engineering level of the station. The overall layout is largely similar to the original, but with the central space converted into a control section.

* Information panels clearly indicate that the cooling system for the primary reactor is offline and consequently running at minimum output.  
  * Hints that the situation is reversible, suggesting intentional sabotage to scuttle the station.  
* Right-hand branch off the central loop reimagined as storage areas with an alien infestation nest area.  
* Increased overall verticality with lifts replacing many of the stairs.  
* Left-hand branch off the central loop reimagined as computer core areas, homage to System Purge level in AB3D1.  
* Reynolds must find the two keys that permit entry to the power plant level to restart the sabotaged reactor.  
* Further alien classes introduced.  
* Additional secret areas to find.

### Level D: System Restart (TODO)

This should be an entirely new level to replace the original. Elements to retain from the original include the first boss and more challenging opponents, i.e. the shotgun guards. The level should include two areas that represent the reactor layout, one operating normally and one overheated.

* Could be an interesting use case for water, with blue texturing and lighting giving a Cerenkov feel.

Reynolds must restore cooling and restart the reactor to stabilise the station. This presents some interesting modding challenges.

* Consider spacing apart several copies of the reactor room that represent the different operational states of a single space  
  * Accessible only by teleports which can be made accessible or inaccessible depending on the stage.  
  * Would need to be spaced far enough apart to ensure the map doesn’t reveal the discontinuity.   
* Alternatively, use a two level room for the reactor space. The upper room can contain the overheated state and the lower one the nominal state.  
  * Access by adjoining single level zones.  
  * Somewhat limits the geometric complexity that can be achieved but simplifies overall integration into the map.  
* The two-level approach could also be used just for peripheral sections of the reactor cooling system and the final reactor area is only revealed after.  
* The restart process should give hints that make it obvious that the station had been deliberately sabotaged and reveal the reason that it was attacked and docked by an alien vessel that was ultimately responsible for the ring damage.  
* The reactor area could be used as a showdown for the boss.

The level progression should result in the original way back to the engineering deck becoming inaccessible and leaving only a push forwards through the attached alien vessel.

### Level E: Exit Strategy (TODO)

This should be an entirely new level. Reynolds must navigate part of the confusing alien vessel in search for a way back to the docking ring or any other route out of the station.

* Level could make some use of elements of the original level, e.g. the required jump.  
* The alien vessel has multiple points of attachment to the station.  
* Eventually finds the surface class teleport in the original station.  
* Remove jetpack from this level.  
* Allow more than one exit route by teleporting to the actual level end zone, the different routes could incorporate different bonuses and challenges.

### Level F: Planetside (WIP)

This is a significant divergence from the original level sequence. Reynolds is teleported to an installation on the surface of LV-612.

* Original Level G, reworked slightly.  
* Needs stronger overall theme and purpose given the designation as a ground to orbit transportation nexus.  
* Learn more about the purpose of LV-612.  
* Should introduce the concept that the planet has multiple remote facilities connected by a teleport network.
